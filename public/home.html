The gap we close

There's no tunnel between Botswana's mobile wallets. So we built the bridge.

01

Every wallet accepted

A buyer on Smega can pay a farmer whose money sits in Orange Money. AgriWise routes the settlement so nobody is turned away at checkout.

02

One balance, three wallets

Farmers see a single consolidated figure and can sweep funds between wallets with transparent fees before they transfer.

03

A record banks accept

Every settled sale builds a verifiable income history — the thing that usually blocks a smallholder's ABSA loan application.

Prototype notice: wallet balances, transfers and checkouts on this site are simulated demonstrations. No real funds move.
